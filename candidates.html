Become a candidate

You don't need to be a career politician. You need conviction, a work ethic, and a community that knows you. Our nomination team will walk you through paperwork, training, and campaign support.

What we ask

  • Canadian citizen, 18 years or older
  • Eligible to vote in a federal election
  • Agreement with the Restore Canada platform
  • Willing to campaign publicly in your riding
  • Pass a standard background and social media review
