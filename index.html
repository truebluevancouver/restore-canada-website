Our principles

What we stand for

Restore Canada is built on four commitments that put people, families, and communities first.

$

Economic Opportunity

We believe every Canadian deserves the chance to build a stable, prosperous life. That means smart jobs policy, affordable housing, and fiscal responsibility.

◆

Secure Communities

Safe neighbourhoods, strong borders, and a justice system that protects victims are the foundation of a free society.

✓

Accountable Government

Canadians deserve transparency, term limits, and elected officials who answer to the people—not to special interests.

✦

National Unity

From coast to coast, we are one country. We respect regional differences while standing together for Canada's future.

Our mission

Rebuilding Canada from the ground up

For too long, Canadians have watched institutions drift away from the people they serve. Restore Canada is a home for citizens who believe our best days are ahead—if we have the courage to reform, renew, and reunite around a common purpose.

We are not a party of nostalgia. We are a party of restoration: renewing the values that made Canada strong, while building the institutions fit for the century ahead.

Our commitments to Canadians

  • Balanced budgets that protect future generations
  • Energy and resource development done responsibly
  • Healthcare and education focused on results
  • Free speech and conscience rights protected
  • A stronger, more independent Canada on the world stage
